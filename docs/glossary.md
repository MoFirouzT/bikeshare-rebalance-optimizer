# Glossary

Terms and symbols used across the docs, defined once.

*Assumes: nothing. This is the entry point for unfamiliar vocabulary.*

---

Terms are borrowed from the field and pinned here, never invented. Each entry says what
the word means *in this project*, and where it helps, what it deliberately does not.
Repurposed common words need this most.

**term**: what it means here. Where the word comes from, if a reader would know it from
another field with a slightly different meaning.

*Gotcha:* the misreading this term invites, and the correction.
